How it works

A reconstruction of VL-JEPA's selective decoding idea from released components.

STEP 1 — WATCH
👁️

V-JEPA 2 encodes motion

A frozen video encoder turns each 16-frame window into visual tokens. It never gets retrained — it just watches.

STEP 2 — PREDICT
🧭

A small head predicts meaning

Our 30M-parameter predictor (the only trained part) maps vision into SONAR — a language embedding space where every point is a sentence.

STEP 3 — SPEAK
💬

Decode only on change

A trigger tracks the predicted meaning over time. Flat line → stay silent. Jump past τ → decode one sentence for the whole segment.
